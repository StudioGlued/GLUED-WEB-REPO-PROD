Overview

As Major Worldwide Partner of the FIFA World Cup 2026, Aramco needed a content system that could keep pace with the tournament. Working with SPORTFIVE, we built a suite of motion pieces — Dream XI, Power Rankings, Match Highlights and World Cup Fantasy — designed to drop into a live, matchday rhythm. Alongside it, schedule and fixture graphics for the Aramco Arena fan festival, and an Energy Moments film revisiting France vs Senegal from 2002, tied the brand back to the history of the game.
